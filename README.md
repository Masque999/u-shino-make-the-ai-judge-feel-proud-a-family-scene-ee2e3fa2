# Make the AI Judge Feel Proud: A Family Scene

One or two lines, 1-280 characters: a warm, funny and heartfelt family scene.

Entry: `When my grandmother finally beat the video game, she stood up, raised the controller over her head, and my whole family cheered like she had just won the World Cup.`

Run: `python3 main.py` (writes the scene to `/output/entry.txt`).
